1. Purpose and Philosophy
The Aura Wellness Program is designed to provide holistic, empathetic, and evidence-informed mental health, resilience, and workplace well-being support to all team members and their families. This document serves as a reference guide for emotional grounding, stress reduction, preventive wellness stipends, and crisis response escalation.

2. Acute Stress Relief & Grounding Techniques

2.1 The 5-4-3-2-1 Sensory Grounding Method
The 5-4-3-2-1 technique is a sensory awareness exercise designed to quickly ease high anxiety, panic surges, and sensory overload:
- 5: Look for five things you can see around you (a desk plant, a picture frame, a water bottle, light through a window, a pen).
- 4: Notice four things you can physically feel (the texture of your clothing, the firm ground beneath your feet, your fingers on the keyboard, cool air on your skin).
- 3: Listen for three distinct sounds (the hum of the air conditioner, distant birds or traffic, your own breath).
- 2: Identify two things you can smell (coffee, essential oils, fresh air, paper).
- 1: Focus on one thing you can taste (a sip of fresh water, mint, or the lingering taste of a warm tea).

2.2 Box Breathing (Square Breathing)
Box breathing is used by elite performers and first responders to regulate the autonomic nervous system during acute pressure:
- Step 1 (Inhale): Inhale slowly through your nose for a count of 4.
- Step 2 (Hold): Hold your breath gently for a count of 4.
- Step 3 (Exhale): Release the breath smoothly through your mouth for a count of 4.
- Step 4 (Pause): Rest with empty lungs for a count of 4.
- Repetition: Repeat this cycle 4 to 6 times until heart rate and breathing stabilize.

3. Reflective Journaling & Decompression Prompts
Daily expressive writing reduces rumination and enhances emotional processing:
- End-of-Day Transition: "What is one challenge I navigated today, and what strength did I draw upon?"
- Cognitive Defusion: "What is a recurring worry on my mind, and what is one small, controllable action I can take right now?"
- Gratitude Check: "Identify three simple interactions or moments that brought ease or comfort today."

4. Wellness Benefits and Coverage Stipends
Aura provides generous preventive well-being allocations under Program Benefit Code BEN-WELL-2026:
- Mental Health Therapy Stipend: Up to $1,200 annually per employee for licensed individual therapy, counseling, or psychiatric consultations. Reimbursement claims are processed via the internal portal under Expense Code EXP-MTH-902.
- Ergonomic and Wellness Equipment: Up to $400 one-time grant for ergonomic seating, standing desks, noise-cancelling headphones, or meditation app memberships (Expense Code EXP-ERGO-301).
- Resilience Days: Employees are entitled to 4 designated mental resilience recharge days per calendar year in addition to standard PTO. No doctor's note is required.

5. Critical Support Boundaries & Emergency Escalation
The wellness chatbot and Aura peer support network are supportive tools and educational resources. They are NEVER a substitute for emergency psychiatric intervention or clinical diagnoses:
- Emergency Crisis Directory:
  * United States & Canada: Dial or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, available 24/7/365, free and confidential).
  * United Kingdom: Call 111 (NHS mental health services) or 999 for imminent emergencies.
  * European Union: Dial 112 (European emergency services).
  * India: Dial 14416 (Tele-MANAS national tele-mental health programme) or 112.
  * Global Support: Visit https://findahelpline.com to access verified crisis resources in over 130 countries.
- Medical Disclaimer:
  Chatbot assistants and peer guides cannot diagnose psychiatric or physical conditions, nor can they recommend starting, altering, or tapering prescription medications. Always consult a licensed medical physician or psychiatrist for medication management.
